About Green Haven

Green Haven is an open garden restaurant celebrating nature, community, and seasonal food. Our tables sit beneath mature trees and fragrant blooms, creating a warm, relaxed dining experience.

We source locally, cook with care, and craft menus that highlight fresh produce and bold flavors.
